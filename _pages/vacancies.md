We are looking for enthusiastic new group members with passion, talent, and curiosity!

You will have the opportunity to work at the interface of statistics, machine learning, and genomics — developing novel methods and tools that address real biological questions in close collaboration with biologists and clinicians.

### Postdoctoral Research Fellows

We support applications for external fellowships and have ongoing positions for outstanding postdoctoral researchers. Please reach out if you are interested.

### PhD Students

Multiple PhD scholarships are available for domestic and international students. We welcome applications from students with backgrounds in statistics, mathematics, computer science, bioinformatics, or related fields.

If you are interested in working with us as a PhD student or postdoc, please send an [email](mailto:heejung.shim@unimelb.edu.au) to Heejung. Please briefly explain why you are interested and attach a CV (including your undergraduate grades). **Please insert _"Application PhD"_ or _"Application Postdoc"_ in the subject line.**

### Master and Undergraduate Students

We welcome project inquiries from Master and undergraduate students at the University of Melbourne. Please contact [Heejung](mailto:heejung.shim@unimelb.edu.au) directly.

For information on Master programs at the University of Melbourne, see the [School of Mathematics and Statistics](https://ms.unimelb.edu.au/study/graduate).
